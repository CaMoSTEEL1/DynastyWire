> # **v0.2.1 — THE v2 OVERHAUL**
> *Everything since v0.1.9. This is the big one.*

> 🏗️ **What v2 actually is**
>
> The app had already run this experiment on itself. The scouting report worked — *"at least the scouting report gets the names right!"* — because **the code works out the facts and the AI only writes the prose around them.** The game recap was the opposite: handed a pile of context and asked to figure out, in writing, who mattered and what it meant. Same AI, same save, same week, opposite results.
>
> So the wrong names and the mixed-up stats were never really an AI problem. They were an architecture problem, and this app had already solved it in one corner. v2 makes that corner the whole app: **the recap, the stakes, the bowl/playoff picture, your history, your coach's résumé and your own playing time are all computed first**, then handed over as facts the writer isn't allowed to argue with.
>
> On top of that, **every piece is fact-checked against your save** — invented people, players on the wrong team, wrong scores, wrong records, stat lines that belong to someone else. You can see the running count at Settings → Fact-check baseline.
>
> The rule underneath all of it: it punishes **contradiction**, never **invention**. A fan can be an idiot about whether you deserved to win. He can't be wrong about who plays for you. Your invented beat writer, the student section, the 3rd-and-8 call, the weather — all still yours.

> 🏈 **ROAD TO GLORY MODE**
>
> Point DynastyWire at an RTG save and it becomes a different app. It knows you're a player, not a coach — the masthead becomes **RoadWire**, covering *the two-star recruit from Georgia* instead of a program.
>
> You're one of eighty-five, and the coverage starts out barely knowing your name. That's the point: the first week the beat writer actually notices you should feel like something.
>
> • **The Week** — your week, written. Including the weeks you don't play, which early on is most of them
> • **His Feed** — a following that grows and shrinks off what you actually do on the field. Post something and see how it lands. Most posts get ignored — that's honest
> • **His Podium** — media as a 19-year-old, where poise, the locker room and how it plays online pull in different directions. Answer in your own words or take one from the room
> • **His Phone** — texts from your position coach, your teammates, and home
> • **His Week** — the real decisions: a hamstring that's "fine", a redshirt window closing, a handler in your DMs, a text from your coach you haven't answered in two days
> • **His Gameplan** — the scouting report as a *player* gets it. Your matchup and nothing else — no team install, no other position group. You're not the coordinator
> • **The Room** — who's ahead of you, ordered by who the staff actually plays
> • **Your Recruitment** — all 138 schools with real offers, read straight from your save. Costs nothing
> • Dynasty and Road to Glory careers are separate lists in the switcher now

> ✨ **New for everyone**
>
> • **The media knows your coach** — titles won, career record, his record at THIS school, how long he's been here, whether he's safe. A two-time champ gets asked different questions than a first-year hire
> • **Year-over-year memory** — from your second season on it remembers: last year's record, revenge games (and it knows when you *won*, so it stops inventing them), rivalries, what a returning player used to be, and the decisions you made
> • **The league's own life** — its headlines, the record book, the coaching carousel, and the man who had your job before you. All read from your save, no credits spent
> • **The app updates itself** — one portable file, no installer, a prompt you can always decline. It confirms the update landed when it comes back, and tells you if it didn't
> • The weekly issue waits while an update is pending instead of writing pages the restart throws away
> • Settings → Fact-check baseline — a real number for how often the Wire gets a fact wrong

> 🐛 **Fixes**
>
> • Fixed: **the AI talking about overalls.** It never sees a rating number now — only football language: all-conference, a quality starter, the weak link
> • Fixed: your **bowl game written as a playoff round.** Bowls are bowls — one game, no bracket, no "first round"
> • Fixed: **unranked teams pushed into the playoff race,** the committee and the New Year's Six. That talk is gone unless you're actually in it
> • Fixed: **"fighting for bowl eligibility"** weeks after you'd already clinched it
> • Fixed: **season totals written as single-game lines** — no more 900-yard, 100-carry games
> • Fixed: **hot-seat talk during a great season.** It reads your real job security now
> • Fixed: a **starter reading as if he wasn't one** on a fresh RTG save
> • Fixed: players on the wrong team and stat lines swapped between them
> • Fixed: Around the League inventing people — one piece named ten players who don't exist
> • Fixed: articles dating things to 2024 instead of your dynasty's year
> • Fixed: pregame press conference answers coming back attached to post-game questions
> • Fixed: the press conference hanging on the loading screen with Skip as the only way out
> • Fixed: the answer buttons at the podium being nearly invisible
> • Fixed: the front page occasionally printing the AI's refusal instead of an article
> • Fixed: a week's issue interrupted mid-write sitting at "writing…" forever
> • Fixed: "under Coach Head Coach" on a Road to Glory save
