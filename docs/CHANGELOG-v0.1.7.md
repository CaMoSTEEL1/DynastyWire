# Dynasty Wire v0.1.7

## 💸 API costs slashed — a season no longer costs what a week did

- A week's issue was burning ~$3 in API credits. That same spend now covers multiple full seasons: budget mode is on by default, and the week's game data is no longer re-billed for every section of the issue — each section after the first reads it at a fraction of the price.
- **New: choose what auto-writes.** Settings → Weekly issue now has a checkbox for every section (Front Page, Social, Rankings, Press Conference, Recruiting, National Desk, Around the League). Uncheck what you don't read and it simply won't generate on its own — but open that tab any time and it still writes itself on the spot. You only ever pay for what you actually use.
- Reopening any past week is still instant and free.
- The "writing this week's issue" popup no longer lists sections stuck on "Queued" forever when you've slimmed down your auto-write list.
- Prefer the richest writing over savings? Flip budget mode off in Settings → Immersion and check every section — that's the old full-fat behavior.

## 🎰 The Book — now with spreads

- **Spread betting is live.** Every game on the board now shows the line ("Kansas State -6.5") and each team's win probability, and you can take the spread at -110 or the moneyline at the listed price — two buttons per team.
- Team records show right on the board, so you know who you're actually backing.
- New **season betting record** next to your bankroll: W-L(-P) and your net, so your degeneracy is fully documented.
- Hover a wager button with a stake entered and it shows exactly what you'd win.
- Open slips now say whether they're a spread or moneyline play.

## 🎙️ Podcast audio actually feels like a podcast now

- No more dead air between lines — the next host's line is recorded while the current one plays, so the show runs back-to-back like a real broadcast.
- Hosts no longer sound like they're reading isolated one-liners — each line is delivered with the flow of the conversation around it, so exchanges land like one continuous recording.
- Restarting or replaying a show no longer spends extra ElevenLabs credits — the audio you already heard is reused.
- **The National Desk radio segment is now listenable too** — same voices, tap play (it never auto-spends your credits).

## 🚨 Suspensions — decisions can now cost a player real games

- **Situations can end in a suspension.** Rarely — a serious legal mess, a flagrant team-rules violation — the fallout of your decision sits a player for 1–3 weeks. It's not just flavor text: the Wire writes it into your save by temporarily dropping his rating so the game itself benches him, then **restores his real rating automatically once he's served it**.
- **Grades have teeth now.** A player on the At Risk academic list can be ruled academically ineligible — it lands on your Situation Room desk as a registrar's letter, the ruling stands no matter what you pick, and he misses the next 2 games. How you handle him and the press is still on you.
- New **Suspended** panel in the Situation Room shows who's out, why, and how many weeks are left; suspended players are badged on the NIL depth chart too.
- **The whole media universe knows.** Every article, presser, social feed, and show is told who's suspended, why, and for how long — writers cover the absence (who steps up, when he's back) with the player's real rating, never a fake "he collapsed to 40 overall" storyline from the temporary bench rating.
- Save writes stay safe: backed up first, verified after, and if the game is running the write just waits until you close it and reopen the Wire.

## 🧑‍🏫 Coach & program polish

- The first letter of your coach's backstory is no longer missing — the drop cap was eating it.
- "Reset Backstory" now confirms in place instead of using a popup that could silently do nothing.
- NIL page: every player's GPA now shows on the roster with Dean's List / At Risk called out, not just a bare number that sometimes didn't appear.
